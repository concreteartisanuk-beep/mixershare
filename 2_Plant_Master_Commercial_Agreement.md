# MIXERSHARE UK – PLANT MEMBER MASTER COMMERCIAL AGREEMENT

**THIS COMMERCIAL AGREEMENT** is made between:

1. **MIXERSHARE UK LTD** ("Platform Operator"), and
2. **THE READY-MIX SUPPLIER / PLANT OPERATOR** ("Plant Member").

---

## 1. PURPOSE & SCOPE OF SERVICE
MixerShare UK operates a managed capacity exchange platform providing Plant Members with access to verified HGV owner-drivers and mixer truck capacity on a per-load radial or shift basis.

---

## 2. RATE STRUCTURE, DYNAMIC EXTENSIONS & PAYMENT TERMS

### 2.1 Standard Rental Rates (£ GBP) & Minimum Order Quota
- **Minimum Order Quota for Spot Relief Work:** To protect supplying owner-drivers against uneconomic vehicle startup, drum wear, and diesel costs, all per-load spot dispatches carry a **strict Minimum Quota of two (2) loads**. Plant Members booking relief mixers on a per-load basis agree to book and pay for a minimum of 2 loads (or the 2-load minimum call-out equivalent).
- **Standard 8m³ Odd Load Radial Spot Relief:** £85.00 base drop fee + £5.00 per radial mile from reporting batch plant to jobsite (covers driver travel diesel & time).
- **2-Axle 4m³ Mini-Mixer Odd Load Spot Relief:** £60.00 base drop fee + £3.75 per radial mile.
- **Half-Day Shift (4 Hours):** £320.00 (Standard 8m³) / £250.00 (4m³) — **100% All-Inclusive (Zero Transfer Fees)**.
- **Full-Day Shift (8 Hours):** £520.00 (Standard 8m³) / £420.00 (4m³) — **100% All-Inclusive (Zero Transfer Fees)**.

### 2.2 Dynamic Shift & Load Extension Protocol
If an ongoing pour requires additional loads or extended shift time beyond the initial booking:
- The Plant Member may request an extension via the portal or dispatch desk:
  - **Extra Load Extension:** Charged at standard odd-load radial rate (£85 base + £5.00/mile for 8m³; £60 base + £3.75/mile for 4m³).
  - **Shift Time Extension:** Charged at £65.00 per additional hour.
- All extensions are logged digitally and automatically added to the final invoice and driver payout.

### 2.3 Platform Transaction Fee & 24-Hour Payout Escrow
- MixerShare UK applies a **10% Service Fee** to all original bookings and authorized extensions (deducted automatically).
- MixerShare UK guarantees **24-Hour Faster Payments deposit** to the supplying owner-driver or lending plant upon digital load completion.

### 2.4 Deadhead Mileage Protection & Minimum Call-Out Policy
To ensure economic sustainability for supplying hauliers and protect owner-drivers against fuel burn on long transit runs:
- **Local Dispatch Threshold (<= 8 Deadhead Miles):** Single and 2-load spot relief bookings are permitted normally at standard odd-load rates.
- **Extended Deadhead Radius (> 8 Deadhead Miles, e.g. 15–20 Miles):** Dispatches exceeding 8 transit miles from the driver's operating base require a **Minimum Call-Out Guarantee**:
  - **Option A:** Booking a Minimum Half-Day Shift (£320.00 All-Inclusive) covering up to 4 hours of plant work.
  - **Option B:** Booking a Minimum of 3 Spot Loads for the assigned truck.
  - **Option C:** Applying a Deadhead Travel Surcharge of £2.50 per mile for miles exceeding the initial 8-mile threshold (e.g., 18 miles deadhead = 10 miles excess × £2.50 = £25.00 surcharge) to cover HGV fuel and transit time.

## 3. STRICT ANTI-CIRCUMVENTION & LIQUIDATED DAMAGES

### 3.1 Non-Circumvention Covenants
Plant Members agree **NOT** to bypass, solicit, or engage supplying owner-drivers or lending plants introduced via MixerShare UK directly offline for a period of **12 months** following an initial dispatch booking.

### 3.2 Liquidated Damages & Enforcement
In the event of a breach of Section 3.1 (bypassing the platform to contract directly offline):
- The Plant Member shall pay to MixerShare UK a liquidated damage fee of **£5,000.00 per unauthorized engagement**, representing agreed pre-estimated commercial damages.
- All umbrella insurance protections, liability indemnities, and network access shall be immediately revoked.

---

## 4. LIVERY & BRAND PROTECTION (WHITE-LABEL POLICY)

### 4.1 Digital Delivery Tickets
All relief deliveries conducted via MixerShare UK shall utilize the platform's **White-Label Digital Delivery Ticket**, bearing exclusively the name, logo, and mix specification of the **Plant Member (Ordering Plant)**.

### 4.2 Magnetic Blanking Panels
Supplying owner-drivers are required to apply neutral magnetic blanking panels over pre-existing truck livery during relief runs.

---

## 5. INSURANCE & COMPLIANCE VERIFICATION

MixerShare UK verifies that all assigned owner-drivers maintain:
- Valid UK HGV Class 2 (Category C) Driving License & Driver CPC Card.
- Minimum £10,000,000 Public & Products Liability Insurance (valid for on-platform dispatches only).
