# MixerShare UK: Step-by-Step North East Launch & National Rollout Plan

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & LAUNCH STRATEGY

MixerShare UK is a managed B2B capacity exchange connecting ready-mix concrete plants (such as CEMEX North East UK and regional independent suppliers) with franchised HGV owner-drivers and idle plant mixer fleets.

---

## PHASE 1: PRE-LAUNCH PREPARATION & COMMERCIAL SETUP (WEEKS 1 – 2)

### 1.1 Secure Legal & Operational Framework
- Finalize the **Plant Master Commercial Agreement** (for CEMEX NE & independent suppliers).
- Finalize the **Owner-Driver Network Agreement** (covering radial mile pay, magnetic blanking panels, and 24hr Faster Payments).
- Order first batch of 50 **MixerShare Neutral Magnetic Blanking Covers** (for owner-drivers to cover existing franchise logos during external relief runs).

### 1.2 Setup Managed Dispatch Portal & Payment Escrow
- Configure the managed dispatch dashboard for North East plant managers.
- Setup UK Faster Payments escrow integration to process 24-hour payouts to owner-drivers upon load/shift completion.

---

## PHASE 2: OWNER-DRIVER RECRUITMENT & CEMEX NE TRIAL (WEEKS 3 – 4)

### 2.1 Owner-Driver Onboarding Drive (North East UK)
- **Target:** Recruit 25 to 40 UK HGV owner-drivers across Teesside, Tyneside, Wearside, County Durham, and Northumberland.
- Distribute the **Owner-Driver WhatsApp / SMS Flyer** directly to driver networks.
- Collect HGV Class 2 licenses, Driver CPC cards, FORS/CLOCS compliance certificates, drum sizes (4m³, 6m³, 8m³), and bank details.

### 2.2 CEMEX North East Hub Trial Launch
- Present the **Plant Manager Pitch Package** to CEMEX North East plant managers (Gateshead, Sunderland, Middlesbrough, Newcastle).
- Onboard initial CEMEX NE batch plants onto the managed dispatch portal.
- Conduct first 10 pilot relief runs to validate dispatch speed, magnetic cover protocol, white-label ticket delivery, and 24-hour driver payouts.

---

## PHASE 3: FULL NORTH EAST COMMERCIAL GO-LIVE (WEEKS 5 – 8)

### 3.1 Expand Independent Plant Onboarding
- Pitch local independent ready-mix producers across the North East (e.g. Tyne & Wear, Durham, Teesside independent yards).
- Activate the **"Independent Trust Shield"** (Blind Delivery Tickets + Non-Solicitation + 24hr Payouts).

### 3.2 Key Operational Metrics & Quality Assurance
- Target 98%+ on-time arrival for emergency radial relief runs.
- Monitor owner-driver feedback and plant satisfaction.
- Collect testimonial quotes from CEMEX NE plant managers and owner-drivers for national marketing.

---
