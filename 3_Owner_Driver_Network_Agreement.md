# MIXERSHARE UK – OWNER-DRIVER NETWORK AGREEMENT

**THIS AGREEMENT** is entered into between **MIXERSHARE UK LTD** ("Platform Operator") and the undersigned **INDEPENDENT HGV OWNER-DRIVER / FRANCHISE HAULIER** ("Owner-Driver").

---

## 1. NATURE OF RELATIONSHIP
The Owner-Driver acts as an independent sub-contractor. Registration on the MixerShare UK network is **100% Free** and does not create an employment relationship. The Owner-Driver retains full freedom to accept or decline dispatch requests.

---

## 2. DRIVER PAYOUT RATES & DYNAMIC EXTENSIONS (£ GBP)

Payouts are deposited directly into your designated UK bank account via **UK Faster Payments within 24 hours** of job completion:

```
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| DISPATCH TYPE            | OWNER-DRIVER PAYOUT RATE                                            |
+--------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Standard 8m³ Odd Load    | £76.50 Base Fee + £4.50 per radial mile (Travel fuel & time covered)|
```

### Dynamic Load & Shift Extensions
If a plant manager asks you to stay for additional loads or extended shift hours on site:
- Tap **"Request Extension"** on your mobile portal or call dispatch.
- Once approved by the plant manager, the extra payout is automatically added to your 24-hour bank transfer!

### 2-Load Minimum Quota Guarantee on All Per-Load Dispatches
MixerShare UK guarantees that owner-drivers will **NEVER be called out to start their mixer for just one (1) load**. All per-load dispatches carry a mandatory **2-load minimum call-out quota** (minimum £171.00 net payout for local drops). If an ordering plant only has 1 load that requires coverage, they are required to pay the 2-load minimum call-out fee to secure your vehicle.

### Deadhead Mileage Protection Guarantee
MixerShare UK guarantees that owner-drivers are protected from uneconomic runs:
- **Local Dispatch Only for Single Loads:** Single spot relief loads are strictly limited to plants within 8 miles of your operating base.
- **Minimum Call-Out Guarantee for Dispatches >8 Miles:** If an ordering plant requires you to travel over 8 deadhead miles (e.g. 15–20 miles), the platform enforces a **Minimum Call-Out Guarantee**: either a guaranteed Half-Day Shift (£288.00 net payout), a minimum of 3 spot loads, or a Deadhead Travel Allowance (£2.50/mile over 8 miles) to fully cover your diesel and travel time.

### Digital Delivery Tickets & Fast Payout Sign-off
After discharging concrete on site:
- Open your mobile delivery ticket (`https://mixershare.co.uk/ticket.html`).
- Log the plant batch ticket number, one-way radial miles, excess waiting time (>30 min billable at £1.50/min), and obtain the customer's finger sign-off on your smartphone screen.
- Submitting the ticket instantly reconciles your payout and triggers your **24-Hour UK Faster Payments bank transfer**.

## 3. STRICT ANTI-CIRCUMVENTION & PROTECTION LAWS

### 3.1 Non-Circumvention Rule
You agree **NOT** to accept offline, direct cash/invoice work from batch plants or jobsites introduced to you via MixerShare UK for a period of **12 months** following a dispatch.

### 3.2 Penalties for Offline Bypassing
If an owner-driver bypasses the platform to deal direct offline:
- **Immediate Loss of 24-Hour Guaranteed Faster Payouts.**
- **Revocation of £10m Umbrella Insurance Cover.**
- **Subject to a £5,000.00 Liquidated Damage Penalty** and permanent removal from the regional exchange network.

---

## 4. OPERATIONAL RULES & BRAND PROTECTION

### 4.1 Magnetic Blanking Covers
When accepting a relief dispatch outside your primary franchise contract, you MUST apply your **MixerShare Neutral Magnetic Blanking Panels** over any existing door logos/livery prior to scale-in at the reporting batch plant.

### 4.2 Non-Solicitation Protocol
You agree **NEVER** to distribute personal business cards, attempt to poach jobsite contractors, or solicit business from contractors introduced via MixerShare UK dispatches.
