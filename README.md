# MixerShare UK — Ready-Mix Fleet Exchange & Plant AI Order Desk

Official website and digital operational tools for **MixerShare UK** (www.mixershare.co.uk) — the UK's first managed B2B Ready-Mix Fleet Capacity Exchange & 24/7 Automated Plant Order Desk.

Founded by **Ian Henry** with 30 years of hands-on UK ready-mix concrete operations experience.

---

## Core Capabilities

1. **🤖 Ready-Mix Concrete Plant AI Sales & Order Desk**:
   - Automated 24/7 telephone receptionist with warm North East UK voice.
   - Gathers customer name, delivery postcode, date/time window, and chute/pump/barrow access.
   - Built-in volume calculator ($L \times W \times D = \text{m}^3$) with mandatory **10% safety margin** for ground compaction & uneven sub-bases.
   - BS 8500 / EN 206 concrete grade advisor (GEN 1, GEN 3, C20, C25, C30 PAV2 air-entrained, RC35, fibre screeds).
   - Delivered radial zone pricing engine + part-load calculations.
   - Instant SMS booking dockets to customer and automated dispatch schedule logging.
   - **Live Test Line:** Call `0191 406 2323` from any UK mobile.

2. **🚚 B2B Ready-Mix & Tipper Capacity Exchange**:
   - Connects independent batch plants with verified HGV owner-drivers for emergency relief loads and peak shift cover.
   - 2-load minimum call-out guarantee (£171+ net) protecting drivers.
   - Automated 24-hour Faster Payments via Stripe Connect UK Escrow.
   - Strict £5,000 anti-circumvention / anti-poaching legal protection.

3. **📱 Digital Shift & Radial Delivery Ticket (`ticket.html`)**:
   - Mobile-first ticket logging for drivers on site: batch dockets, one-way radial miles, waiting time, customer touch sign-off, and instant WhatsApp sync.
