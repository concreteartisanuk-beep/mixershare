## 🎯 THE INDUSTRY NEED

Standard UK driving schools teach drivers how to pass their HGV Class 2 test in a dry, stable rigid box wagon. They teach **zero practical skills** for operating a ready-mix concrete truck.

When inexperienced drivers jump into a mixer:
- **Rollover Risk:** Wet concrete has a high, moving centre of gravity that shifts violently on roundabouts and curves.
- **Catastrophic Drum Hardening:** If an engine cuts out or a driver mismanages drum rotation, concrete can set inside the drum, resulting in a **£15,000+ replacement or jackhammer repair**.
- **Slump & Cube Failure Liability:** Uncontrolled site water additions ruin concrete strength, leading to failed test cubes and massive contractor claims.
- **Environmental Fines:** Washing out chutes into storm drains can trigger **up to £10,000 in Environment Agency fines**.

MixerShare UK solves this by providing hands-on, practical training from an operator with 30 years in the cab.

## 📚 1-DAY INTENSIVE COURSE MODULES

### Module 1: Hands-On Roadcraft & Liquid Surge Driving (In-Cab Behind-The-Wheel)
- 1-on-1 behind-the-wheel driving instruction with an experienced 30-year veteran.
- Managing rotational inertia and dynamic liquid weight shift on roundabouts (understanding UK passenger-side drum rotation surge physics), dual carriageway bends, and off-camber curves.
- Safe braking distances and progressive stopping techniques with up to 20 tonnes of live moving concrete.

### Module 2: Batch Plant & Site Reversing Mastery
- Blindside reversing under the batch tower loading cone without mirror strikes or plant damage.
- Precision weighbridge positioning and scale-in/scale-out etiquette.
- Navigating tight residential housing developments, soft ground, trench edges, and backing onto concrete pumps.

### Module 3: Drum Mechanics, Slump & Water Liabilities
- In-cab and rear external drum fin controls: charging, agitating, and discharging speeds.
- **Zero-Slump Rules & The Unwritten Law:** The crucial operational rule for transporting dry and semi-dry mixes that prevents catastrophic internal "balling" and drum-packing.
- Visual identification of S1, S2, S3, and S4 slump consistencies.
- Slump loss during transit (weather, distance, temperature effects).
- "Customer Added Water on Site" disclaimers, test cube failures, and legal liability protection.

### Module 4: Chute Safety, Environmental Washout & Emergency Stalls
- Ergonomic chute extension mounting (preventing severe back strain and pinch-point crush injuries).
- Washout protocols: compliant use of site wash pits and truck wash-down systems.
- **The Correct Way to Stone Out for Maximum Effect:** The veteran technique for end-of-shift scouring after hauling dry or sticky loads, protecting drum blades and preventing 1–2 tonnes of lost payload.
- Zero-tolerance Environment Agency compliance (preventing £10,000 drainage pollution fines).
- Emergency protocols: immediate steps if engine or PTO stalls under load to prevent catastrophic drum hardening.
